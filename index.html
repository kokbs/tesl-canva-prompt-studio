1Know your pupils

A useful prompt identifies exactly who will use the site and what language support they need.

Include differentiation, language scaffolds or accessibility needs.

2Define the learning

Keep the experience focused on one achievable English learning outcome.

Use one observable verb such as identify, choose, arrange, compare or produce.

3Plan meaningful interaction

Interaction should require pupils to use the language, not merely click through information.

4Shape the learning environment

Specify a consistent visual style and essential accessibility features.

Prompt-writing coach

Use these questions while making each decision.

👥
Who are the learners?Age, proficiency and support needs determine language, layout and pacing.
🎯
What will they demonstrate?Write one observable outcome rather than listing several broad goals.
🧩
What will they do?Choose an interaction that produces evidence of language learning.
💬
How will feedback help?Explain why an answer works and guide pupils towards improvement.
♿
Can everyone participate?Check contrast, readability, keyboard use, audio alternatives and motion.
